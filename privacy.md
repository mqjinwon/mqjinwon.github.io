# Privacy

This page describes a local tool that runs on Jinwon Kim's own computers. The Google OAuth app name is Jinwon Kim.

## Google data

The tool can read and change data in the Google account that Jinwon Kim authorizes:

- Gmail
- Calendar
- Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides
- Contacts
- Tasks

It uses that data to prepare his own notes, schedule, and files. It does not sell the data. It does not use the data for ads. It does not share the data with a third party.

## Storage

OAuth tokens and copies of the data stay on his computers. The public website does not receive the contents of his Google account.

## Revoke

He can remove the app at [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
